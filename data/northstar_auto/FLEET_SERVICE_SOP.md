# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Commercial Fleet Maintenance & Intake

**NorthStar Auto Care** — *Precision Diagnostics. Honest Service. Road-Ready Reliability.*

Downtime costs a business money. This SOP sets out what fleet customers can expect from us, and what we need from you.

## 1. Priority Triage & Guaranteed Turnaround (SLA)

Fleet vehicles are placed ahead of retail customers in the bay queue, with safety-critical repairs and customer emergencies taking precedence over both.

| Service Class | Examples | Guaranteed Turnaround |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preventative Maintenance (PM Tier 1)** | Oil and filter, tire rotation, DVI | **Same-day (under 4 hours)** for drop-offs before **08:30 AM** |
| **Minor Mechanical Repairs** | Brakes, alternators, starters | **24-hour** turnaround window |
| **Major Drivetrain Overhauls** | Transmission, engine, differential | Dedicated bay allocation with **daily status progress dispatch** |

- **Clock Start:** The SLA clock starts when the vehicle is logged into our shop management software, and when diagnostic approval is received if the repair requires authorization above the pre-authorized limit.
- **Parts Delays:** If a part is not available locally, we source it from a second supplier. We notify the fleet manager within **2 hours** of discovering a delay, with the new estimated completion time.
- **SLA Exceptions:** Delays caused by awaiting approval, hidden damage found during teardown (see `ESTIMATE_AND_AUTHORIZATION_POLICY.md`), or manufacturer back-orders pause the SLA clock.
- **Severe-Duty Notice:** Vehicles with GVWR above 14,000 lb are outside the light-fleet scope. Call us to discuss them.

## 2. Morning Dispatch Rules

- Morning dispatch begins at **06:30 AM** with the early-shift dispatch technician logging after-hours drop-offs and arranging the day's bay assignments.
- **Priority Order:** (1) Safety-critical (RED) fleet repairs, (2) PM Tier 1 drop-offs received before 08:30 AM, (3) scheduled minor repairs, (4) other fleet work, (5) retail customers.
- Fleet managers receive a text and email with a work-order number for each unit by **07:30 AM**.
- Fleet DVI reports are issued within **90 minutes** of drop-off, with photos and a color grade for each of the 40 inspection points.
- Pickup updates: Units are staged in the Fleet North Lot, and keys are available at the front desk from 7:00 AM.

## 3. After-Hours Fleet Key Drop & Vehicle Hand-off

1. Secure vehicle in designated Fleet North Lot parking stalls.
2. Complete the Fleet Work Order envelope detailing unit ID, current odometer, and primary driver complaint.
3. Deposit key into the tamper-evident dual-locking night drop safe located at the Bay 1 exterior wall.
4. Early shift dispatch technician logs intake scans into shop management software at **06:30 AM**.

**Notes**
- Lock the vehicle and remove valuables, fuel cards, and tools.
- Any vehicle dropped after-hours is treated as received at 06:30 AM for SLA purposes. It does not qualify for the 08:30 AM same-day PM guarantee unless it was in the lot before 08:30 AM and logged by 06:30 AM, which is the standard case.
- NorthStar is not responsible for items left in vehicles.
- Our lot is lit and under camera surveillance.

## 4. Pre-Authorized Spending Thresholds

- Fleet managers designate a **pre-authorized repair limit (standard: $350.00)** to expedite workflow without waiting for written authorizations.
- Repairs at or under the limit proceed immediately after the DVI. They are documented on the invoice.
- Repairs **over the limit** need written authorization through the SMS or email approval link from a designated authorized contact. Alternatively, call the fleet desk.
- The limit applies **per vehicle, per visit**, not per line item, and cannot be split to avoid authorization.
- Limits can be raised in writing, up to $1,000.00 for approved Net-30 accounts, and can be set per unit ID.
- Safety-critical RED items are always reported to the fleet manager, even if under the limit.

## 5. Commercial Credit & Invoicing

- **Terms:** Net-30 invoicing upon credit review approval. Credit review takes up to 3 business days.
- **Consolidated Monthly Statements:** Itemized billing grouped by vehicle VIN and internal company fleet unit ID. Statements issue on the **1st business day** of each month for the previous month's work, due 30 days from the statement date.
- **Late Balances:** Accounts more than 15 days past due may lose priority scheduling until the account is current. A 1.5% monthly finance charge applies after 30 days.
- **Disputes:** Raise invoice questions within 14 days of the statement date.
- **Warranty:** Fleet repairs receive the same 24-month/24,000-mile warranty as retail repairs, subject to the severe-duty exclusions in `WARRANTY_AND_REPAIR_POLICY.md`.

## 6. Preventative Maintenance Runbooks

Each fleet account receives a scheduled PM calendar built on `service_intervals_matrix.json`, with reminders sent **14 days** before a unit is due. Units that go more than 1,000 miles past due are flagged on the monthly statement.
