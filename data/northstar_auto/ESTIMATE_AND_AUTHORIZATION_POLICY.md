# ESTIMATE & AUTHORIZATION STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

**NorthStar Auto Care** — *Precision Diagnostics. Honest Service. Road-Ready Reliability.*

No surprises. You see what we see, you approve each item, and you never pay for work you didn't authorize.

## 1. Digital Vehicle Inspection (DVI) Color Grading

Every vehicle undergoes our **40-point** visual and mechanical digital inspection before work begins. Technicians take photos and short videos of what they find.

- **GREEN (Pass):** Component within manufacturer operating specifications; no action required.
- **YELLOW (Future Attention):** Component shows moderate wear (e.g., brake pads at 4mm–5mm, slight valve cover seepage); monitor for next service cycle.
- **RED (Immediate Safety Risk):** Component failed or poses immediate safety/drivability danger (e.g., brake pads below 2mm, torn CV boots, coolant pressure leak); requires prompt repair.

### Reference Measurements

| Item | GREEN | YELLOW | RED |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Brake pad thickness | Over 5 mm | 4–5 mm (about 3 mm is the trigger for replacement on many vehicles, and the technician explains it) | Under 2 mm |
| Tire tread depth | 6/32 in. or more | 4/32 to 5/32 in. | Under 4/32 in. in winter (see `WINTERIZATION_AND_PREVENTATIVE_GUIDE.md`); under 2/32 in. is the legal minimum |
| Battery (CCA conductance) | 100% to 70% of rated CCA | 70% to 50% | Under 50% or failed load test |
| Coolant freeze point | -34°F (-37°C) or lower | -20°F to -33°F (-29°C to -36°C) | Warmer than -20°F (-29°C) |
| Brake fluid (moisture) | Under 2% water | 2% to 3% | Over 3% water or boiling point below 311°F (155°C) |

*Thresholds can vary by vehicle manufacturer specification, and the manufacturer's value always takes priority.*

## 2. Estimate Authorization & Supplemental Disclosures

- **No Verbal Guesswork:** Written estimates are delivered via SMS and email with embedded inspection photos.
- **Explicit Customer Approval:** No repair work commences without digital or physical written signature authorization.
- **Teardown Supplements:** If hidden mechanical damage is discovered post-disassembly, work stops immediately, and a formal supplement estimate is dispatched.

### SMS Digital Authorization Workflow
1. The service advisor sends the estimate by SMS (secure link) and email, listing each line item with parts, labor hours, tax, and the total.
2. The customer opens the link and sees the DVI photos and notes for each item.
3. The customer **approves or declines each line item individually**, then signs digitally.
4. The system timestamps the approval and sends a confirmation message with the authorized total.
5. If we haven't heard from you in **2 hours**, we call. We never begin work on silence.
6. Customers without a smartphone can approve in person or by phone with the advisor, and we record the call with consent.

### Supplemental Teardown Approvals
- If more work is found, the technician stops, photographs the issue, and the advisor sends a **supplement estimate** by SMS.
- Supplements follow the same approval workflow, and the original authorization does not cover them.
- If the supplement is declined, we reassemble the vehicle. Teardown and reassembly labor already performed is billed at the quoted rate, and we explain it before starting.
- **Price Variance Rule:** The final bill won't exceed the authorized total by more than 10% or $50.00 (whichever is less) without a new written approval.

### Estimate Validity
Estimates are valid for **14 days**. Parts prices may change after that.

## 3. Customer Part Inspection Rights

Under state automotive repair regulations, customers retain the right to inspect and keep all replaced mechanical parts (excluding exchange items subject to manufacturer core charges, which may be inspected on-site before return to distributor).

- Ask for your old parts when you authorize the work, and we will bag and tag them at the front desk.
- Core-charge items (alternators, starters, brake calipers, and batteries) can be viewed on-site, but must go back to the distributor. Core charges are listed on the estimate.
- Parts that are hazardous (batteries, fluids) will be handed over in sealed containers or disposed of in compliance with the rules.
- Unclaimed parts are kept for **7 days** after pickup of the vehicle.

## 4. Declined Work

Declined RED items are documented on your invoice with a photo and signed acknowledgment. We never pressure customers, and YELLOW items are only noted for your future planning.
