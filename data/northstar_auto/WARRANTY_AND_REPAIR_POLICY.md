# WARRANTY COVERAGE & REPAIR POLICY

**NorthStar Auto Care** — *Precision Diagnostics. Honest Service. Road-Ready Reliability.*

## 1. NorthStar 24-Month / 24,000-Mile Nationwide Peace of Mind Warranty

NorthStar Auto Care warrants all parts and labor performed by our ASE-certified technicians for **24 months or 24,000 miles**, whichever occurs first.

- **Coverage:** Mechanical parts replacement failure, installation labor defects, and hydraulic seal leaks. Both **parts and labor** are covered at no charge for the repaired component.
- **Nationwide Protection:** Valid at over 35,000 certified participating repair centers across North America. If you are more than 25 miles from NorthStar, call the warranty hotline before authorizing any repair so the claim can be validated.
- **Exclusions:** Commercial severe-duty abuse, auto racing, lack of subsequent routine fluid maintenance, customer-supplied parts, and normal brake pad/wiper friction wear.

### Clear Examples

| Covered | Not Covered |
| :--- | :--- |
| A water pump we replaced starts leaking at 14 months | Brake pads worn thin after 18,000 miles of normal driving |
| A caliper seal leaks within warranty | Wiper blades that streak |
| A starter we installed fails because of a defective part | A repair on a part you supplied |
| A serpentine belt tensioner we installed was misinstalled | Failure caused by skipping oil changes or ignoring a DVI RED item |
| A loose connection from our labor | Damage from racing, off-road use, or collision |

## 2. How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Stop driving the vehicle if the problem is a safety issue (brakes, steering, overheating).
2. Call NorthStar or the nationwide warranty hotline with your original invoice number.
3. Bring the vehicle to NorthStar or a participating facility. A technician will diagnose the failure. If it is covered, you pay $0 for parts and labor.
4. Keep your invoice and the maintenance records for your vehicle, because the warranty requires evidence of routine fluid maintenance.

If we need to diagnose a failure that turns out not to be covered, the diagnostic fee applies (see Section 3).

## 3. Diagnostic Fee & Labor Rate Structure

- **Initial Diagnostic Fee:** **$145.00 flat fee** covers comprehensive OBD-II scanner telemetry, pinpoint electrical circuit testing, and up to **1 hour** of physical teardown analysis.
- **Diagnostic Credit Rule:** *100% of the $145.00 diagnostic fee is credited directly toward repair labor* if the customer authorizes the recommended repair service.
- **Standard Labor Rate:** $145.00 per hour, billed on the flat-rate labor times in `service_intervals_matrix.json` and published guides. You are billed for the labor time quoted and not the clock.
- **Additional Diagnostic Time:** Beyond the first hour, diagnostic work is billed at $145.00 per hour only after you approve the additional time.
- **Credit Window:** The credit applies if the repair is authorized within **30 days** of the diagnostic visit.
- **Diagnosis Isn't a Guarantee of Repair Cost:** The diagnostic tells you what's wrong. You then receive a written estimate (see `ESTIMATE_AND_AUTHORIZATION_POLICY.md`).

## 4. Customer-Supplied Parts Policy

To maintain our strict safety standards and warranty integrity, NorthStar Auto Care *does not install customer-supplied parts*. All parts are sourced through OEM dealership networks or Tier-1 aftermarket suppliers carrying manufacturer warranties.

- We can't verify the quality, fitment, or origin of parts we did not source, and we can't warrant labor on a part with no warranty chain.
- We will always tell you whether a quoted part is OEM or aftermarket, and show you the price difference.
- If you bring a part, we will gladly diagnose the problem, but you will need to use our parts for the repair.

## 5. Parts & Labor Warranty Limits

- Parts are warranted at the level of the manufacturer or longer. If the manufacturer's warranty is shorter than 24 months, NorthStar covers the remainder of our 24 months or 24,000 miles.
- **Consequential Damage:** Towing, rental cars, lost time, and secondary damage are not covered, except where required by law.
- Where state law gives you greater rights, the law applies.

## 6. Shop Liability

NorthStar carries garage liability insurance. Report any damage that occurred while your vehicle was in our care **before leaving the lot**, or within 24 hours, and we will investigate.
