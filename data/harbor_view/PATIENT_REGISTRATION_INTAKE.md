# NEW CLIENT & PATIENT REGISTRATION INTAKE POLICY

**Harbor View Veterinary Clinic** — *Compassionate Medicine. Modern Care. Lifelong Companionship.*

We want your first visit to be calm, prepared, and fear-free. This policy explains what we need from you and why.

## 1. Medical History & Record Transfer Requirements

- **Advance Records Submission:** Prior medical charts, surgical notes, and vaccination certificates must be received at least **24 hours prior** to the initial consultation. Send them by email, fax, or through the client portal, or ask your previous clinic to transfer them directly.
- **Unverified Vaccines:** Animals without verifiable veterinary clinic medical records will be placed on an age-appropriate booster catch-up schedule to guarantee safety. A rabies certificate from a licensed veterinarian is the only accepted proof of rabies status.
- **If Records Are Late:** The visit goes ahead as scheduled, but the veterinarian may have to repeat vaccines or tests and cannot make decisions based on information we have not seen.
- **What to Bring:** A fresh fecal sample (under 12 hours old), a list of current medications and supplements, and any pet insurance information.

## 2. Appointment Booking Deposit & No-Show Policy

- **New Client Deposit:** A non-refundable **$65.00 deposit** (applied directly toward the exam fee) is required to secure the initial booking slot.
- **48-Hour Cancellation Window:** Deposits are transferable to a rescheduled date if notice is provided at least **48 hours** prior to the appointment. Cancellations under 48 hours forfeit the deposit.
- **No-Shows:** A no-show forfeits the deposit. A second missed appointment requires the full exam fee at the time of booking.
- **Medical Exception:** If your pet has an emergency and was treated at another hospital, tell us and we will review the circumstances with compassion.
- **Fees:** The deposit is a credit toward the visit and is never an extra charge. Itemized treatment estimates are always provided before any diagnostics or procedures.

## 3. Fear-Free Arrival Protocols

- **Canine Patients:** Must remain secured on a non-retractable 6-foot nylon leash at all times. Please do not allow dogs to greet one another.
- **Feline Patients:** Must be transported in a secure, hard-sided plastic carrier lined with a familiar blanket. Towel coverings sprayed with Feliway pheromones are available at the front entrance. Please do not remove your cat from the carrier in the lobby.
- **Small Animals and Other Pets:** Must travel in an escape-proof carrier.
- **Separate Suites:** We maintain separate canine and feline reception and exam areas, with Feliway and Adaptil diffusers.
- **Reactive Patient Parking Check-In:** Guardians of anxious or dog-reactive pets may call reception from their vehicle at **(555) 438-2323** to be ushered directly into an exam room, bypassing the lobby. Please remain in your car until a team member confirms the room is ready.
- **Pre-Visit Calming:** Ask us if your pet may benefit from prescribed pre-visit anxiety medication, given at home the night before and/or 1 to 2 hours before the visit. Never give human medication.
- **Treats:** Bring your pet's favorite treats and tell us about any allergies.

## 4. Consent, Estimates & Payment

- A veterinarian reviews findings and provides an itemized estimate before any procedure. Written consent is required.
- Payment is due at the time of service. We accept major credit and debit cards, and we can help you review third-party financing and pet insurance reimbursements.

## 5. Aggressive or Fearful Patients

If your pet has a history of biting or extreme fear, tell us when you book. This is not a reason to turn you away. It lets us plan low-stress handling, muzzle conditioning, or pre-medication in advance.
