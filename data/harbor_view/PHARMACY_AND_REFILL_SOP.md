# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Pharmacy Dispensing & Prescription Refills

**Harbor View Veterinary Clinic** — *Compassionate Medicine. Modern Care. Lifelong Companionship.*

## 1. Veterinary-Client-Patient Relationship (VCPR) & Legal Mandates

Under state veterinary licensing regulations and FDA compliance:

- **Annual Wellness Exam Requirement:** A physical examination performed within the preceding **365 days** is legally required to dispense or approve any prescription medication. No exceptions can be made by front-desk staff.
- **Establishing a VCPR:** The veterinarian must have recently examined the patient in person, have enough knowledge of the animal to diagnose its condition, and be available for follow-up. Telephone or chat alone cannot establish a VCPR.
- **Therapeutic Blood Monitoring:** Chronic maintenance medications require periodic laboratory auditing before refills:
  - *NSAIDs (Carprofen/Meloxicam):* Chemistry panel (BUN, Creatinine, ALT) every 6 to 12 months.
  - *Thyroid / Phenobarbital / Incurin:* Serum concentration monitoring every 6 months.
  - *Heartworm preventives:* Annual canine antigen test before refilling (see `preventive_care_matrix.json`).
- **Controlled Substances (e.g., gabapentin where state-scheduled, buprenorphine, phenobarbital):** Refills are limited by state rules and require a current exam. They are never refilled by phone the same day.

## 2. Prescription Processing Turnaround

- **Standard In-House Refills:** Require **48 hours (2 business days)** notice for clinical review, technician calculation, and doctor authorization.
- **Compounded Specialty Formulations:** Require **5 business days** for compounding lab preparation.
- **Urgent Same-Day Requests:** If a pet will run out of a critical medication (seizure, cardiac, or thyroid medication) within 24 hours, the technician escalates to the attending veterinarian, who may dispense a short bridge supply (up to 7 days) if the VCPR is current.
- **Refill Request Channels:** Online portal, phone at (555) 438-2323, or in person. Requests left after 4:00 PM count as the next business day.

### Refill Workflow
1. Receptionist logs the request and verifies the date of the last exam (365-day rule).
2. Technician verifies the medication, weight, and the date of the last monitoring labs.
3. If labs or an exam are overdue, the client is contacted with options and an itemized estimate.
4. Veterinarian authorizes the refill.
5. Pharmacy technician fills it and runs a double-check on the weight-based dose (mg/kg). Client is notified by text or phone.

## 3. Weight-Based Dosing Verification

- Dogs and cats are weighed at every visit, and the dose is recalculated whenever the weight has changed by more than 10%.
- Reference example: carprofen at 2.2 mg/kg every 12 hours. A 20 kg dog receives 44 mg per dose, rounded to the nearest practical tablet fraction as approved by the veterinarian.

## 4. Third-Party Online Pharmacy Authorizations (Chewy, 1-800-PetMeds)

To prevent medication counterfeit risks and improper storage, Harbor View does not approve external third-party digital faxes directly. Clients may pick up a complimentary written physical prescription to submit directly to their pharmacy of choice.

- Paper prescriptions are issued only when the VCPR is current and, for monitored drugs, labs are current.
- Written prescriptions are valid for **12 months**, or less where state law specifies.
- **Fee:** Complimentary written prescription. Fees for the recheck exam or lab work remain as quoted.
- Staff must never state that external pharmacies are untrustworthy. We simply explain that we cannot verify the supply chain of products we do not dispense.
- Controlled substances cannot be sent electronically to a third-party online pharmacy.

## 5. Client Communication Standards

- Be empathetic: pets on chronic medication are often elderly or ill, and guardians may be worried.
- Offer clear estimates before labs. Never block a refill silently. Always explain why and what is needed.
- Cases involving suspected adverse reactions (vomiting, black stool, lethargy) are escalated to a technician or veterinarian immediately.
