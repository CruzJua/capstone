# PROPERTY ACCESS, SECURITY & CANCELLATION POLICY

**Kettle & Loom Cleaning Collective** — *Pure Ingredients. Meticulous Spaces. Uncompromising Clean.*

We are entrusted with your home or workplace. This policy explains how we handle access, and what happens when plans change.

## 1. Secure Access & Key Storage Protocols

* **Digital Keypads & Smart Locks:** Preferred access method. Clients provide a unique service PIN designated exclusively for Kettle & Loom staff. We recommend a PIN that is active only during the service window.
* **Physical Lockboxes:** If using physical keys, keys must remain inside a secure on-site lockbox. Technicians will not carry permanent property keys off-site. Lockbox codes are shared only with the assigned team and changed on request.
* **Alarm Systems:** Alarm disarm codes and re-arming instructions must be stored in encrypted client profiles. Any false alarm dispatch fees resulting from inaccurate client codes are the client's responsibility.
  - Technicians disarm on arrival and re-arm on departure. If an alarm triggers, the technician calls the client and the alarm company within **5 minutes** and gives the correct passcode.
  - Provide your alarm company's name and the verbal passcode for dispatch.
* **Departure Procedure:** Technicians re-lock all doors, close windows, set the thermostat to its original setting, turn off lights, and text a completion notice.
* **Key Custody:** Access details are visible only to the Operations team and the assigned technicians. Access codes are removed from the technician's device when the contract ends.
* **Garage and Gate Codes:** Provide any gate, garage, or elevator access in your profile.

## 2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Cutoff Schedules

* **48-Hour Free Cancellation:** Services may be canceled or rescheduled without penalty up to **48 hours** prior to the scheduled arrival window.
* **Late Cancellation (Within 24–48 Hours):** Incurs a **$50.00 administrative rescheduling fee**.
* **Same-Day Cancellation / Lockout (Under 24 Hours):** Charged at **100% of the scheduled service fee**. Our collective technicians are scheduled and paid for their booked time.

| Notice Given | Fee |
| :--- | :--- |
| More than 48 hours | $0.00 |
| 24 to 48 hours | $50.00 |
| Under 24 hours | 100% of scheduled service fee |
| Lockout on arrival | 100% of scheduled service fee |

**Cancellation Details**
- Notice is counted from the start of your scheduled arrival window.
- Cancel through the client portal, by phone, or by email. Messages received after business hours (6:00 PM) count as received the next business morning.
- **Weather & Emergencies:** If unsafe travel conditions or a documented emergency prevent service, we reschedule without a fee.
- **Short-Term Rental Turnovers:** Cancellations of a turnover tied to a booking that was itself canceled by a guest are free when notified within 24 hours of the guest's cancellation.

## 3. Lockouts

A lockout occurs when technicians cannot enter the property within **20 minutes** of the start of the arrival window because of an incorrect code, a missing key, a deactivated PIN, or a locked interior door.

- The technician tries the client's contact numbers and waits up to 20 minutes.
- If entry is still not possible, the visit is recorded as a lockout and billed at **100% of the scheduled service fee**.
- A one-time free rebooking is not offered for lockouts, but we are glad to help get the next visit set up.
- Lockouts due to a building-management error are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## 4. Pet Safety & Containment Rules

We love pets, but for technician safety and to prevent pets from darting out exterior doors, dogs and cats must be secured in a designated room or crate during service unless prior written arrangements are confirmed.

- Mark the room with a sign, and make sure the room has water.
- Tell us about any pets that bite, escape, or react to strangers when you book.
- Technicians may decline to enter a property where a dog is not secured, and the visit is treated as a lockout.
- Exotic pets, reptiles, and aquariums are not touched or cleaned.
- Pets must not be left in spaces with wet floors.

## 5. Property Protection & Damage Claims

- Report any damage within **24 hours** of service. Photos are required.
- Kettle & Loom maintains general liability insurance and bonding. Claims are reviewed within 5 business days.
- Fragile and valuable items (jewelry, cash, and collectibles) should be secured. Technicians do not move items worth over $250 unless asked.

## 6. Contact

Reach the scheduling team through your client portal or by phone during business hours.
