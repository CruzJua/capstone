# TECHNICAL GUIDE: Post-Construction Fine Dust Remediation

**Kettle & Loom Cleaning Collective** — *Pure Ingredients. Meticulous Spaces. Uncompromising Clean.*

Construction dust is fine, clings to surfaces, and is easily disturbed. Drywall gypsum, sawdust, and, in some cases, crystalline silica (from cutting concrete, tile, or masonry) require a methodical, top-to-bottom process.

## 1. The Three-Phase Construction Remediation Methodology

Post-construction cleaning requires a sequential three-phase approach to capture settling airborne drywall gypsum and sawdust:

1. **Phase 1: Rough Debris Pass:** Removal of construction packaging, protective films, tape residue, and rough floor sweeping with HEPA capture.
2. **Phase 2: High-to-Low HEPA Vacuuming:** Dry particle extraction starting from ceiling joists, HVAC diffusers, door casings, down to baseboards.
3. **Phase 3: Wet Microfiber Sparkle Pass:** Multi-bucket damp wipe using electrostatic microfiber cloths to capture remaining microscopic dust films.

### Phase Details

| Phase | Typical Labor Share | Key Tasks | Do Not |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Rough Debris | 20% | Remove films, stickers, tape, and packaging; scrape paint splatter with plastic blades; sweep with HEPA-equipped machines or use damp mopping | Dry sweep with a standard broom, which re-suspends dust |
| 2. High-to-Low HEPA | 40% | Ceiling fans, light fixtures, vents, tops of cabinets, window tracks, shelves, then walls, then baseboards, then floors | Use a household vacuum without HEPA, or vacuum low areas first |
| 3. Wet Microfiber | 40% | Wipe all horizontal and vertical surfaces, fixtures, glass, and cabinet interiors | Use a wet cloth on thick dry dust; vacuum first |

- Work **high to low** and **back to front**, and finish at the entrance.
- Allow dust to settle for at least **24 to 48 hours** after the last drywall sanding or cutting before the final pass. We usually schedule Phase 2 and Phase 3 as separate visits.
- Typical labor estimates are in `service_scope_matrix.json` (about 240 minutes per 1,000 sq ft for a full three-phase clean).

## 2. HEPA Air Scrubbing & Particle Trapping Standards

* **Equipment Standard:** Commercial HEPA vacuums certified to capture **99.97% of particulates down to 0.3 microns**. Certification labels must be on the machine, and filters are inspected before every project.
* **Filter Maintenance:** Replace or clean HEPA filters when the indicator shows full, or at least every **40 hours** of use. Empty bags and canisters outdoors or into a sealed bag at the end of every shift, away from the work area.
* **Air Scrubbers:** For projects with heavy dust, place a HEPA air scrubber (rated at 4+ air changes per hour for the room volume) in the room during Phase 2, and keep it running for 4 hours afterward.
* **Microfiber Fold Technique:** Microfiber cloths must be folded into **8 clean quadrants** and flipped continuously to prevent spreading dry drywall dust across painted walls.
  - When all 8 quadrants are soiled, the cloth goes in the used pile. Never dip a used cloth back in the clean solution.
  - Use the multi-bucket system: one bucket of clean solution, one of rinse, and fresh cloths from a clean stack.
  - Use **damp**, not wet, cloths, wrung out so that no water drips. Wet cloths turn drywall dust into a paste.
* **Surface Cleaner:** OxyPure, diluted to label, or plain water on delicate finishes. See `CHEMICAL_SAFETY_AND_SDS_GUIDE.md`.

## 3. Worker Safety

- **Respiratory protection:** NIOSH-approved N95 at minimum, and a P100 half-face respirator when silica may be present.
- **Eye protection and gloves** are always worn. Disposable coveralls are used for heavy dust.
- **Silica:** Where crystalline silica from cutting stone, concrete, or tile is a concern, work is limited to wet methods and HEPA only. Do not dry sweep. Follow OSHA's respirable crystalline silica standard.
- **Hydration and breaks:** 10-minute break every 90 minutes in dusty areas.
- Our technicians are not trained for asbestos or lead abatement. If suspected materials are found, work stops in that area, and the client is informed.

## 4. Quality Verification

- **White Glove Test:** A clean white microfiber cloth wiped across a horizontal surface at **3 sample locations per room** should pick up no visible dust.
- **Flashlight Inspection:** A raking-light check of painted surfaces and glass at a low angle.
- **Walkthrough:** The client or general contractor does a final walkthrough with the Site Lead, with a punch list completed within **48 hours**.
- HVAC: Replace the system filter after the final pass. Ducts are not cleaned under this service.

## 5. Scheduling

| Stage | Timing |
| :--- | :--- |
| Rough clean (Phase 1) | After trade work, before final painting touch-ups |
| Final clean (Phases 2–3) | After all trades are done, no earlier than 24–48 hours after last sanding |
| Touch-up visit | Within 7 days of the final clean, if needed |

Dust can resettle for several days after finishing, which is why a short touch-up visit is offered.
