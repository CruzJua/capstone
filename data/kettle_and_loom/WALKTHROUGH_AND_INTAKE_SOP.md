# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Property Walkthrough & Client Onboarding

**Kettle & Loom Cleaning Collective** — *Pure Ingredients. Meticulous Spaces. Uncompromising Clean.*

Every property is different. We learn yours before we clean it.

## 1. 5-Step Property Walkthrough Intake Protocol

1. **Initial Property Audit:** Conduct a **30-minute** on-site assessment with the client or property manager. Measure and record the square footage of each room and total conditioned space, and note the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and floor types. Confirm that the square footage on the booking is within 10% of the measured figure.
2. **Surface Sensitivity Mapping:** Log all sensitive materials (honed Carrara marble, unsealed butcher block, oil-rubbed bronze hardware, wool area rugs). Each entry has the room, material, and the approved product (see `CHEMICAL_SAFETY_AND_SDS_GUIDE.md`).
3. **Pre-Existing Condition Documentation:** Photograph existing carpet stains, scratched wood floors, and chipped tiles. Upload to the client profile with a date stamp. Take at least **3 photos per room** (wide shot plus close-ups), and a photo of each existing defect with a coin or ruler for scale.
4. **Air Quality & Allergen Assessment:** Identify presence of pets, dust sensitivities, or fragrance allergies to tailor hypoallergenic essential oils or go fully unscented.
5. **Estimate & Scope Confirmation:** Generate a fixed-price proposal detailing initial deep-clean baseline hours and ongoing maintenance frequencies. The estimate uses `service_scope_matrix.json` and a base rate of $55.00 per technician-hour.

**Output:** A signed Property Profile containing the sensitivity map, photo log, access and alarm details (stored encrypted), pet details, and scope.

## 2. Mandatory Initial Deep-Clean Requirement

All recurring service contracts must undergo a foundational **Initial Deep Clean**. This baseline visit removes pre-existing buildup, limescale, and synthetic wax residues from previous chemical cleaners before beginning botanical maintenance.

- **Duration:** Typically **2.0x to 2.5x** the standard maintenance time for the same square footage (for example, a 2,000 sq ft home that takes 150 minutes for maintenance will take about 5 to 6.25 hours of technician time for the deep clean).
- **Added Tasks:** Interior oven and refrigerator, baseboards, door casings, window sills and tracks, cabinet interiors on request, descaling of fixtures, and detailed grout work.
- **Why It Is Mandatory:** Botanical chemistry cannot lift old synthetic residues on its own, and the baseline sets a measurable standard for every visit after it.
- **Short-Term Rental Properties:** A baseline deep clean is required before the first turnover.
- **Pricing:** Quoted as a fixed price at the walkthrough. Rates are not charged beyond the quote unless conditions differ from the photos, and the client approves any change first.

## 4. After the Walkthrough

- The proposal is sent within **1 business day**.
- Services start no sooner than 3 business days after signing, so we can schedule the same technician team.
- A named lead technician is assigned. We aim to keep the same team on each visit.

## 5. Photo & Privacy Handling

Photos are used only to document condition and are stored in the client's secured profile. They are deleted 90 days after the contract ends, unless a dispute is open.
