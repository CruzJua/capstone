# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Chemical Safety & Botanical Chemistry Guidelines

**Kettle & Loom Cleaning Collective** — *Pure Ingredients. Meticulous Spaces. Uncompromising Clean.*

This SOP supports OSHA Hazard Communication and EPA compliance. It tells our technicians what we use, how long to leave it on a surface, and what to do if something goes wrong. Botanical does not mean harmless, and we handle every product with care.

## 1. Approved Botanical Formulations & Active Ingredients

Kettle & Loom exclusively deploys EPA Design for the Environment (DfE) and Green Seal certified cleaning chemistry:

| Product / Formulation | Active Botanical Base | Target Pathogens / Soils | Dwell Time Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bio-Shield Hospital Disinfectant** | Thymol (0.05% botanical thyme extract) | 99.99% bacteria, enveloped viruses, fungi (per label claims) | 10 minutes (wet dwell) |
| **OxyPure Multi-Surface Cleaner** | Hydrogen Peroxide (stabilized 3%) | Soap scum, organic grease, protein soils | 3 minutes |
| **Citrus-Loom Mineral Descaler** | Organic Citric Acid (food-grade 8%) | Hard water scale, calcium deposits, rust | 5 minutes |
| **PureLoom Floor Wash** | Saponified Coconut & Olive Oil Soap | Hardwood, engineered plank, stone tiles | Immediate mop dry |

### Dwell Time Rules
- **Wet dwell** means the surface stays visibly wet for the whole period. If it dries early, reapply.
- Pre-clean heavy soil first, because disinfectants do not work through grease or grime.
- Do not wipe Bio-Shield before 10 minutes have passed. Use a timer.
- Disinfection is performed on high-touch surfaces only: toilets, handles, switches, keypads, faucets, and remotes.

### Dilution & Mixing Rules
- **Never mix** products with one another. Never combine hydrogen peroxide with vinegar or other acids, because they form peracetic acid, an irritant.
- **Never** mix with chlorine bleach or ammonia brought by a client.
- Dilute concentrates only with the metered dispensers, using room-temperature water (60°F–80°F / 16°C–27°C). Hot water above 120°F (49°C) degrades hydrogen peroxide and thymol.
- Use fresh dilutions. Discard unused mixed solutions after **7 days**.

## 2. Stone & Surface Material Compatibility Warnings

* **Strict Acid Prohibition:** *Citrus-Loom Descaler and acidic solutions must NEVER be applied to unsealed natural marble, travertine, limestone, or terrazzo.* Acid etching permanently damages calcite stone.
* **Hardwood Neutrality:** Wood flooring must be cleaned exclusively with pH-neutral PureLoom wash. Never saturate it with standing water.
* **Granite & Quartz:** Use OxyPure only. No descaler on sealed stone unless the sealer is confirmed intact and the surface is rinsed within 5 minutes.
* **Oil-Rubbed Bronze & Unlacquered Brass:** Dry microfiber only. No acids or peroxide, as they strip the finish.
* **Unsealed Butcher Block:** Damp wipe with PureLoom diluted to the label ratio. Do not apply disinfectant to unsealed wood for a full dwell time.
* **Wool Rugs & Natural Fibers:** Do not apply hydrogen peroxide, because it may bleach dyed fibers. Spot test first in a hidden area.

Always check the client's Surface Sensitivity Map (see `WALKTHROUGH_AND_INTAKE_SOP.md`) before opening a product.

## 3. SDS Exposure Protocols & First Aid

Every product has a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) in the technician binder and the mobile app, readable offline.

| Exposure | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Inhalation** | Low-hazard, zero-VOC formulations. Move to fresh air if anyone has irritation and ventilate the room. Seek medical attention if symptoms last more than 15 minutes. |
| **Eye Contact** | In case of direct splash, flush eyes with potable water for **10 minutes**, holding the eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if easy to do. Seek medical attention if irritation continues. |
| **Skin Contact** | Rinse with water for **5 minutes**. Remove contaminated clothing. Seek medical advice if redness persists. |
| **Ingestion** | Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Call Poison Control at **1-800-222-1222** (U.S.) and follow their directions. |

**Note:** Concentrated 3% hydrogen peroxide and 8% citric acid can irritate eyes and skin. Wear nitrile gloves and eye protection when pouring or diluting concentrates.

### Incident Workflow
1. Make the area safe and give first aid.
2. Notify the Site Lead immediately.
3. Record the product, time, and persons exposed in the incident log within 1 hour.
4. Report to the Operations Director within 24 hours.
5. If a client or client's pet is affected, contact the client the same day.

## 4. Storage & Dilution

- All botanical concentrates must remain in labeled, UV-resistant amber dispensers at **50°F–80°F (10°C–27°C)**.
- Never store in vehicles overnight, and never decant into unlabeled bottles or food containers.
- Keep stored products out of direct sunlight and away from heat sources.
- Rotate stock. Hydrogen peroxide has a shelf life of about 12 months from the opening date, and open dates must be written on the bottle.
- Dispose of spent solutions down a sanitary drain with running water. Never pour them into storm drains.

## 5. Client Safety Disclosures

- Clients with fragrance sensitivities, asthma, or chemical allergies are recorded at intake. We use unscented formulations on request.
- Pets and infants should be kept out of a room while a surface is wet. Surfaces are safe for contact once dry.
- We list ingredients by name and provide SDS copies to any client on request.
