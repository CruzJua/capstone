# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Allergen Safety & Cross-Contact Protocols

**Cinder & Salt Bakery** — *Wild Yeast. Heritage Grain. Honest Craft.*

Radical allergen transparency is one of our core values. This document tells staff exactly what to do, and tells customers exactly what we can and cannot promise.

## 1. Airborne Flour & Celiac Safety Notice

Cinder & Salt Bakery operates an open-concept bakery and an active stone mill. We mill whole grains (wheat, rye, spelt, einkorn, Red Fife) on-site every week.

- **Gluten Presence:** Fine flour particles remain airborne throughout the facility 24 hours a day. No wall, bench, or schedule fully removes them.
- **Celiac Disclaimer:** While some items are prepared without gluten-containing ingredients, **our facility is NOT celiac-safe**. We do not produce certified gluten-free products. Customers with Celiac Disease, severe gluten intolerance, or wheat allergy should not consume our products.
- **Fermentation Is Not a Safety Claim:** Our 24–36 hour cold fermentation breaks down some starches and phytic acid and makes bread gentler for many people. It does **not** reduce gluten to a celiac-safe level (the regulatory threshold is under 20 ppm). We never market it as doing so.
- **Staff Script:** "We're an active stone mill, so flour is in the air all day. We can't make a celiac-safe promise, and we'd rather tell you that honestly than risk your health."

## 2. Allergen Matrix & Kitchen Separation

The following major allergens are present in our production facility:

| Allergen | Present in Facility | Dedicated Equipment Used | Storage Protocols |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gluten / Wheat / Rye** | Yes (Primary) | No | Open bin and mill-hopper storage |
| **Tree Nuts** | Yes (Walnuts, Almonds) | Yes (Color-coded Red) | Sealed lug containers, top shelf of dry storage |
| **Sesame** | Yes | Yes (Color-coded Yellow) | Labeled, lidded ingredient bins |
| **Dairy** | Yes (Butter, Milk, Cheese) | Yes (Color-coded Blue) | Dedicated refrigeration shelf |
| **Eggs** | Yes | Yes (Color-coded Blue) | Bottom-shelf refrigeration |
| **Soy** | No intentional use | N/A | Not stocked; supplier statements on file |
| **Peanuts** | No intentional use | N/A | Not stocked; may be present in neighboring facilities' supply chain, so no guarantee is made |

**Separation Rules**
1. Color-coded tools (bowls, scrapers, bench knives, proofing baskets) never leave their station.
2. Sesame and nut tools are washed separately and never share a sink basin with plain-dough equipment during the same cycle.
3. Staff change aprons and wash hands to the elbow for 20 seconds when moving from a seeded or nut station to a plain station.
4. Shared equipment (deck ovens, spiral mixers, mill) cannot be allergen-dedicated. Cross-contact is possible and disclosed.

## 3. Daily Sanitization & Production Sequence Runbook

To minimize cross-contact, production follows a strict sequence:

| Time | Task | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **02:00 AM** | Pure / Seedless Bakes | Plain sourdoughs (Country White, Spelt Boule) shaped and baked first, in freshly cleaned baskets. |
| **04:00 AM** | Enriched & Seeded Bakes | Seeded loaves (Seeded Rye, Sesame Baguettes) and nut pastries are processed second. |
| **12:00 PM** | Mid-Day Sanitation | Scrapedown and sanitization of all mixers, benches, and proofing baskets. |
| **08:00 PM** | End-of-Shift Washdown | 3-compartment sink wash with 200 PPM quaternary ammonium sanitizer, steam sanitization of racks, HEPA vacuuming of mill housing. |

**Verification**
- The Shift Lead initials the sanitation checklist at 12:00 PM and 08:00 PM.
- Sanitizer concentration is tested with test strips before every use. Target is 200 PPM, with a 1-minute contact time.
- Oven decks are brushed between seeded and non-seeded loads (bake temperature 475°F / 246°C).
- Mill housing is HEPA-vacuumed. Compressed air is never used because it aerosolizes flour.

## 4. Labeling & Customer Communication

- Every retail item carries a label with ingredients and a "Contains" statement.
- A posted notice at the counter and on the website reads: *"Our facility is not celiac-safe. All items may contain traces of wheat, tree nuts, sesame, dairy, and eggs."*
- Customers with allergies are never told an item is "safe". They are told what is in it and what is nearby.
- Any question we cannot answer from the allergen matrix is passed to the Head Baker, and no guess is offered.

## 5. Allergen Incident Escalation Workflow

1. **Halt Production:** Flag the affected batch with RED "ALLERGEN CONTAMINATED" tape and stop the line.
2. **Log Incident:** Record batch number, time, and cross-contact source in the Kitchen Shift Log.
3. **Repurpose or Dispose:** The batch cannot be sold under standard labeling. Relabel it internally as staff product with a full allergen declaration, or discard it.
4. **Notification:** Inform the Head Baker and Quality Lead before shift conclusion.
5. **Customer-Facing Incident (Reported Reaction):**
   - Advise the customer to seek medical attention immediately, and call 911 for breathing difficulty or anaphylaxis.
   - Record the product, purchase time, and batch code.
   - Escalate to the Quality Lead within 1 hour. Pull and quarantine remaining units from the same batch.
6. **Root-Cause Review:** Within 72 hours, the Quality Lead documents the cause, the corrective action, and any retraining.
