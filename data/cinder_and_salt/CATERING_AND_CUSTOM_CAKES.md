# CUSTOM EVENTS & BESPOKE CAKE ORDERING POLICY

**Cinder & Salt Bakery** — *Wild Yeast. Heritage Grain. Honest Craft.*

From rustic wedding tables to corporate brunches, we bake with heritage grain and patient fermentation. Please read this policy before booking so we can set your event up for success.

## 1. Booking Lead Times

- **Weddings & Large Events (100+ guests):** Minimum **6 weeks** notice required. Peak-season dates (May–October) close up to 6 months in advance.
- **Bespoke Celebratory Cakes:** Minimum **14 days** notice required.
- **Small Wholesale Event Trays:** Minimum **7 days** notice required.
- **Rush Requests:** We cannot accommodate bookings inside these windows. Our sourdough cake layers and laminated doughs need 18–36 hours of fermentation, and decorating takes more time on top of that.

## 2. Tasting Consultations & Box Pickups

- **Fee:** $45.00 per tasting box (includes 4 sourdough cake options and 3 seasonal buttercreams/preserves).
- **Credit:** The $45.00 fee is credited toward any final catering contract exceeding **$300.00**.
- **Pickup Schedule:** Boxes are prepared on the 1st and 3rd Saturday of every month and are available for pickup between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM.
- **Reservation:** Tasting boxes must be reserved at least 7 days in advance. Unclaimed boxes are not held past 1:00 PM.
- **Allergen Note:** Tasting boxes are produced in our standard facility. Tree nuts, dairy, eggs, wheat, and sesame may be present. Our facility is **not celiac-safe**.

## 3. Deposit, Payment & Cancellation Schedule

- **Deposit:** 50% non-refundable deposit required at contract signing to reserve the event date.
- **Final Balance:** Remaining balance must be paid in full 14 days prior to the event date.
- **Cancellations:**
  - 30+ days prior: deposit retained.
  - 14–29 days prior: 50% of contract value retained.
  - Under 14 days: 100% of contract value retained.
- **Guest Count Changes:** Final guest count is due 14 days before the event. It can increase by up to 10% within that window, subject to capacity. It cannot decrease after that date.

## 4. Temperature Handling & Outdoor Setup Rules

- **Temperature Threshold:** Swiss meringue buttercream softens at 72°F (22°C) and collapses above 80°F (27°C).
- **Outdoor Display Limit:** Outdoor displays in temperatures above **72°F (22°C)** are strictly limited to **2 hours maximum**.
- **Direct Sunlight:** Cakes must remain in complete shade. Direct sunlight induces collapse within 15 minutes.
- **Hot-Weather Alternative:** For events forecast above 80°F (27°C), we recommend a naked or fondant-finished cake, or a cake displayed indoors, and we will help you choose.
- **Storage Before Service:** Cakes finished in buttercream should be held in a refrigerator at 38°F–41°F (3°C–5°C) and brought to room temperature for 45–60 minutes before service.

## 5. Delivery Logistics

- **Delivery Fee:** $35.00 within Zone A (Urban Core) and $65.00 within Zone B (Outer Metro). Locations beyond our zones are quoted individually.
- **Delivery Window:** We deliver and set up during a 2-hour window agreed in your contract, finishing at least 90 minutes before service.
- **Venue Requirements:** A level table, shade, and a clear loading path. A refrigerated space within the venue is strongly recommended for tiered cakes.
- **Tiered Cakes:** Cakes of three or more tiers are assembled on site by a staff member, and an on-site fee of $100.00 applies.
- **Equipment:** Stands, boards, and linens are provided on loan with a refundable $75.00 deposit. They must be returned within 5 days.
- **Closed Mondays:** Delivery and pickup are not available on Mondays.

## 6. Allergen Transparency for Events

We cannot guarantee any event item is free of wheat, tree nuts, sesame, dairy, or eggs. Please share all guest allergies in your intake form. We will give you full ingredient lists and cross-contact notes so you can plan, and we will tell you plainly if an item is not appropriate for a guest. See `ALLERGEN_SAFETY_PROTOCOL.md`.
