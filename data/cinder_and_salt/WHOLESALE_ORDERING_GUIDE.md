# WHOLESALE PARTNER PROCUREMENT & DELIVERY GUIDE

**Cinder & Salt Bakery** — *Wild Yeast. Heritage Grain. Honest Craft.*

Welcome, chefs, café owners, and grocers. This guide explains how we work together. Our bread takes time, so our schedule is firm.

## 1. The 48-Hour Fermentation Lead Time Rule

Our sourdough breads undergo a mandatory 24-to-36-hour cold fermentation cycle. Because we use no commercial yeast or dough accelerants, **all wholesale orders require a strict 48-hour advance notice**.

- **Standing Order Freeze:** Standing orders lock at **2:00 PM** two days prior to delivery.
- **Order Modifications:** Additions or reductions must be submitted through the B2B Portal before the 2:00 PM cutoff. Late modifications cannot be accommodated, because the dough is already mixed and committed.
- **No Same-Day or Next-Day Orders:** We do not offer rush orders at any price.

| Order Placed By (2:00 PM) | Fermentation & Proofing Window | Delivery Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sunday** | Monday | **Tuesday Morning** |
| **Monday** | Tuesday | **Wednesday Morning** |
| **Tuesday** | Wednesday | **Thursday Morning** |
| **Wednesday** | Thursday | **Friday Morning** |
| **Thursday** | Friday | **Saturday Morning** |
| **Friday** | Saturday | **Sunday Morning** |

*Note: Retail counter and delivery routes are closed on Mondays. Orders placed on Saturday before 2:00 PM are treated as placed for Monday and are not deliverable. They roll to the Sunday cutoff and deliver Tuesday.*

## 2. Minimum Order Values & Regional Routes

- **Minimum Order Value (MOV):** $75.00 per delivery drop. Orders below $75.00 incur a $15.00 small-drop surcharge.
- **Delivery Schedule:** Tuesday through Sunday, 4:30 AM – 7:00 AM.
- **Delivery Zones:**
  - **Zone A (Urban Core):** daily drops, Tuesday–Sunday.
  - **Zone B (Outer Metro):** Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday drops only.
- **Route Timing:** Drivers give a 15-minute arrival text. Within a window, routes run in a fixed order set by the dispatcher.

## 3. Pricing Tiers

- **Tier 1 – Standard:** Default wholesale pricing for all approved accounts.
- **Tier 2 – Volume:** Applies once an account averages **$600.00 or more per week** over a trailing 4-week period. Example: Country Sourdough Boule is $6.25 at Tier 1 and $5.75 at Tier 2 (retail $9.50).
- Current price lists live in the B2B Portal and follow `daily_bake_matrix.json`.

## 4. Unassisted Delivery & Storage Protocols

- **Access Requirements:** Partners must supply a reliable keycode or lockbox key for entry.
- **Drop Location:** Drops are made directly to the partner's designated walk-in cooler or food-safe staging counter. Fresh bread is placed in breathable paper or cloth bags, never sealed plastic.
- **Cold Weather Protection:** From Nov 1 to Apr 1, exterior lockbox drops are strictly prohibited to prevent thermal shock. A staffed or interior access point is required.
- **Refrigeration Warning:** Do not refrigerate bread. Cold storage speeds staling (see `BREAD_STORAGE_AND_REVIVAL.md`).

## 5. Invoicing, Credit Terms & Quality Claims

- **Credit Application:** New accounts submit a credit application and are approved before first delivery. Approval typically takes 3 business days.
- **Payment Terms:** Net-15 via automated ACH through the wholesale portal. Invoices generate upon delivery departure.
- **Late Payment:** Balances more than 15 days past due pause deliveries. A 1.5% monthly late fee applies after day 30.
- **Quality Claims:** Missing, damaged, or unsatisfactory items must be reported within **12 hours of delivery drop** with cross-section photos. Approved claims are credited to the next invoice.

## 6. Allergen Obligations for Partners

Our facility is **not celiac-safe**, and we mill on-site. Partners reselling our products must pass along our allergen statement (see `ALLERGEN_SAFETY_PROTOCOL.md`) and must not describe our products as gluten-free or allergen-free.
