# B2B WHOLESALE & COMMERCIAL FLEET PROCUREMENT SOP

**Iron & Oak Provision Co.** — *Built for the Trail. Proven for a Lifetime.*

For crews, outfitters, and retailers who need gear that holds up. This SOP is for account managers and commercial partners.

## 1. Tiered Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

| Tier | Quantity (units per order) | Discount off MSRP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | 25–99 units | 40% |
| **Tier 2** | 100–499 units | 48% |
| **Tier 3** | 500+ units | 55% |

- A "unit" is one finished item (one coat, one belt, one pair of pants). Mixed SKUs may be combined to reach a tier.
- Orders of fewer than 25 units are not wholesale and are handled through retail or a gift program.
- Tier pricing is locked for **12 months** from account approval, and Tier 2 and 3 accounts can set blanket orders with scheduled releases.
- **Sample Program:** Up to 3 sample units are available at Tier 1 pricing, credited back if a first order of 25+ units is placed within 60 days.

## 2. Account Approval

1. Submit the commercial account application (business name, EIN, resale certificate or tax-exempt form if applicable, and trade references).
2. Credit check and review: **3 to 5 business days**.
3. Account manager assigned and portal access granted.

Tax-exempt buyers must provide a valid certificate before invoicing. Orders shipped without a certificate are taxed where applicable.

## 3. Custom Branding & Debossing Guidelines

Hot-stamp custom logo debossing is available across all leather goods. Co-branded apparel patches are available for Tier 2 and 3 accounts.

- **Artwork:** Vector files (.AI or .PDF) are required. Raster files (JPG/PNG) are not accepted.
- **Tool-and-Die Fee:** A one-time **$150 tool-and-die brass mold fee** applies per custom artwork submission. Reorders using the same die are free.
- **Size Limits:** Maximum deboss area is **2.5 in. × 1.25 in.** on belts and **3 in. × 3 in.** on notebooks and wallets. Minimum line weight is 0.5 pt (1 mm gap between elements).
- **Stamp Temperature:** Stamps are applied at roughly **230°F–260°F (110°C–127°C)**.
- **Proof Approval:** A digital proof is sent within 3 business days of artwork receipt, and a physical sample deboss is available for $35.00. Production starts only after written proof approval.
- **Brand Compliance:** We reserve the right to decline artwork that infringes a trademark or conflicts with our brand.
- Custom branded goods are **final sale**.

## 4. Payment Terms & Invoicing

- **Standard Terms:** **Net-30** upon approved credit evaluation. New accounts may begin with a credit limit of $5,000 and increase after 6 months of on-time payment.
- **Customized Batches:** **50% deposit** is required on custom co-branded production batches, with the balance due Net-30 from shipment.
- **Payment Methods:** ACH, wire, or check. Credit cards are accepted for orders under $2,500 with a 3% processing fee.
- **Late Payments:** A 1.5% monthly fee applies after 30 days. Accounts more than 45 days past due are placed on hold.

## 5. Lead Times & Fulfillment Logistics

- **Manufacturing Lead Time:** Standard **4 to 6 weeks** production window from deposit receipt (or PO approval) to freight delivery.
- **In-Stock Items:** Ship within 5 business days.
- **Custom Co-Branded Orders:** 4 to 6 weeks after proof approval and deposit.
- **Freight:** LTL freight for orders over 150 lb. Small-parcel ground for smaller shipments. Freight is prepaid and billed on the invoice, or the customer may use their own carrier.
- **Freight Free Threshold:** Tier 3 orders ship freight-free within the contiguous U.S.
- **Receiving:** Inspect and note damage on the bill of lading. Report shortages within **7 days** of delivery.

## 6. Wholesale Returns & Warranty

- Wholesale orders are **final sale** except for manufacturing defects reported within 30 days.
- The Heritage Guarantee covers the end user. Defective units may be repaired under `REPAIR_POLICY.md`, or credited against a future order.
- Retail partners must not describe products or the guarantee in ways that differ from our published terms.

## 7. Escalation

Disputes, credit holds, and large custom projects are escalated to the Wholesale Account Manager within 1 business day.
