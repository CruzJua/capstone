# RETURNS & EXCHANGES POLICY

**Iron & Oak Provision Co.** — *Built for the Trail. Proven for a Lifetime.*

If it doesn't fit or doesn't suit, send it back. No runaround.

## 1. 45-Day Field Trial Guarantee

Test your gear for up to **45 days** from the delivery date. Items returned in **unwashed, original condition with tags attached** receive full refunds to the original payment method.

**Eligible:**
- Unwashed and unaltered. Light try-on and trail testing is fine.
- Original tags attached.
- Returned within 45 days of delivery.

**Not Eligible:**
- Washed, soiled, stained, or damaged items. (If gear fails from defects, use `REPAIR_POLICY.md` instead.)
- Items with pet hair, strong odor, or smoke.

## 2. Return Workflow

1. Open the customer portal and select **Start a Return** with your order number.
2. Choose Return or Exchange and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label (domestic) or show the QR code at a carrier drop-off.
4. Pack the item with tags and ship within 7 days of generating the label.
5. Track your return through the portal.

- **Domestic Returns:** Return shipping is deducted from refunds ($7.95 flat), except for exchanges and defective items, which are free.
- **International Returns:** Customer pays return shipping, and duties and taxes are not refundable.

## 3. Exchanges & Digital Return Labels

Complimentary prepaid digital shipping labels are generated automatically via our customer portal for all domestic size and color exchanges.

- We ship the replacement when the carrier shows your original item in transit **if** your account is in good standing, or when we receive it.
- If the replacement costs more, we charge the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference.
- One free exchange per item. Further exchanges carry a $7.95 shipping charge.
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your size is out, we refund in full.

## 4. Final Sale Exclusions

The following are non-returnable and excluded from refund policies:

- Custom monogrammed leather goods.
- Altered sleeve hems and any other custom tailoring.
- Factory seconds.
- Archived sale batches, labeled "Final Sale" at checkout.
- Gift cards.

Monogrammed or hemmed items are still covered by the Heritage Guarantee for defects.

## 5. Restocking Fees & Processing

- **Zero restocking fees.**
- Return inspections are processed within **3 business days** of warehouse receipt.
- **Refund Turnaround:** Refunds are issued to the original payment method on approval. Cards typically show the refund within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on your bank.
- You get an email once your return has been inspected.

## 6. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If you receive the wrong item or one damaged in transit, contact us within **14 days** of delivery with photos. We ship a replacement or refund in full, with free return shipping.
