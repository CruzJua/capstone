# REPAIR POLICY: The Iron & Oak Heritage Guarantee

**Iron & Oak Provision Co.** — *Built for the Trail. Proven for a Lifetime.*

We build gear to be fixed, not thrown out. If it fails from honest field use, we repair it. Here's exactly what that means.

## 1. Iron & Oak Heritage Guarantee Scope

The guarantee covers the **lifetime of the product** for the original purchaser and for any later owner who can show the item's SKU and a serial/lot stamp.

| Covered Under Guarantee | Excluded / Non-Covered |
| :--- | :--- |
| Structural seam splits and blown stitching | Normal wear, cosmetic patina, and surface scuffs |
| Broken brass rivets, snaps, and buckles | Mold, pet damage (chewing, scratching), and extreme chemical exposure |
| Zipper teeth failure and runner separation | Unauthorized third-party modifications or repairs |
| Leather hardware failures (belt keepers, strap loops, buckle tongues) from regular field use | Deliberate abuse, prolonged submersion, and extreme neglect |
| Detached pockets, ripped gussets, and failed bar-tacks at stress points | Fading, water-line marks, and wax loss (see `CARE_GUIDE.md`) |

### Exclusions in Plain Terms
- **Patina is character, not a defect.** Scratches, darkening, creases, and fading are part of the gear's life.
- **Neglect:** Gear left wet and balled up for weeks, mold growth, or leather cracked from never being conditioned is not covered. We will still quote you a paid repair.
- **Abuse:** Cutting, burning, using a coat as a tow strap, or hitting a belt with an industrial tool is not "regular field use".
- **Third-party work:** A tailor re-stitching a seam voids coverage for that seam only. The rest of the item stays covered.

## 2. 5-Step Repair Submission Workflow

1. **Photo Triage:** Submit clear photos of the damage (close-up plus full item, in daylight) through the customer intake portal.
2. **RMA Issuance:** Receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number within **24 hours** (business hours, Mon–Fri). Write the RMA number on the outside of the box.
3. **Incoming Shipping:** Ship the gear to our bench workshop. The customer covers incoming shipping.
4. **Bench Repair:** Our craftsmen repair the item using original heavy-gauge thread and solid brass hardware. Average repair cycle is **10–14 business days** from receipt.
5. **Return Shipping:** The restored item is returned with complimentary ground freight (typically 3 to 7 days, depending on where you are).

**Before You Ship**
- Empty the pockets and remove personal items.
- Do not wash the garment. Dirty is fine. Washing can hide or worsen the damage.
- Pack in a sturdy box or mailer. We recommend tracked shipping with insurance.

- **Repair Time:** 10–14 business days is the average. Peak periods (October through December) can add up to **5 additional business days**, and we will tell you when you get your RMA.
- **Updates:** You receive an email on receipt, when repair begins, and when the item ships.
- **Unclaimed Items:** Items that remain unclaimed 60 days after repair notification are donated or recycled.

## 4. Non-Warranty (Paid) Repairs

If an item falls outside the guarantee, we will send you a quote **before** any work begins. You approve, or we return the item unrepaired. Typical repair price ranges:

| Repair | Price Range |
| :--- | :--- |
| Seam re-stitch (up to 12 in.) | $15–$30 |
| Zipper replacement | $45–$75 |
| Rivet or snap replacement (each) | $5–$10 |
| Belt buckle or keeper replacement | $20–$40 |
| Leather re-conditioning and re-dye | $35–$60 |

Quote approval window is 10 days. Quotes are binding for 30 days.

## 5. Beyond Repair & 40% Trade-In Policy

When structural repair would compromise safety or integrity, or the item is damaged beyond functional repair, the customer receives a **40% trade-in credit** toward a replacement item.

- Credit is based on the **current retail price** of the replacement item and applies to a single purchase.
- Credit is valid for **12 months**.
- We recycle usable brass hardware and leather offcuts.
- Trade-in credit is available for items outside the guarantee as well, at the discretion of the bench lead.
- The decision on repair versus replacement is made by a bench craftsperson, and we will explain it with photos.
